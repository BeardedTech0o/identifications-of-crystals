On-device, offline, no account needed

How this works

This app does not use any cloud AI or paid service. Everything runs on your device:

  • A free, on-device machine learning model (TensorFlow.js + MobileNet) looks at your photo's visual texture and helps rule out obvious non-crystals.
  • Your camera photo is analyzed for dominant color and edge/facet patterns using plain image processing.
  • You answer a few quick questions (transparency, luster, color) about what you see.
  • All of this is combined into a best-effort match against a reference database of 117 common crystals and minerals.

This is a heuristic tool, not a lab-grade identification method. For valuable or unusual specimens, consult a professional geologist or gemologist.

No reference photo library is bundled with the app (to respect image copyright), so matches are based on described traits rather than photo comparison. Each crystal's info page shows a representative color swatch instead of a photo.

Everything — your photos, scan history, and the ML model once downloaded — stays on your device and works fully offline after first launch.
